# FormProof video review

Use this public contract when a future analysis service turns one training clip
into a user-facing report.

1. Read the requested movement and user-provided load, repetitions, RIR/RPE,
   pain, and filming angle as separate training records.
2. Verify that the visible exercise matches the requested movement. If it cannot
   be verified, stop the technical assessment and request a clearer clip.
3. Check video duration, dimensions, orientation, decoder status, occlusion, and
   whether one complete repetition is available.
4. Select representative stages from one complete repetition. Do not combine
   unrelated repetitions to create a cleaner story.
5. Put safety before performance. Pain, obvious loss of control, or high-risk
   loading prevents an increase recommendation.
6. Label evidence confidence as high, medium, or low and explain the reason.
7. Write every important finding as observed evidence, why it matters, and one
   next action.
8. Keep visible technique, user-entered workload, historical comparison, and
   muscle-growth claims separate.
9. Do not infer muscle growth from fewer than four completed same-user,
   same-category sessions.
10. Never diagnose injury. Recommend qualified in-person help when appropriate.

Public examples must be synthetic or explicitly approved for redistribution.
Never include real private videos, screenshots, reports, credentials, absolute
local paths, or production prompts in this repository.
